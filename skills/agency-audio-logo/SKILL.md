# Audio Logo

A 7-second answer to *"What do you do?"* — sharp enough to drop at networking events, on podcasts, in DMs, and in the first 60 seconds of every discovery call. **Pillar 1** of the Rockstar Advisor system.

## Inputs

Read:
- `memory/mvv.md` — for voice and reason-for-being
- `memory/icp.md` — to anchor on a recognizable buyer
- `memory/offer-stack.md` — to anchor on the actual outcome

## Goal

Produce 3-5 audio logo options, each:

- **Under 7 seconds spoken aloud** (≈ 18-22 words)
- **Concrete buyer + concrete outcome** — never generic
- **Memorable hook** — something repeatable a third party could say back
- **No corporate dead-words** — no "leverage", "synergy", "unlock", "transformation"

## Method

Use the **For X / Who Y / I Z** structure as a starting frame, but allow it to break. Three formulas to try:

### Formula 1 — For/Who/I
*"For [ICP] who [pain trigger], I [verb] [outcome]."*
Example: *"For consultants stuck doing $200/hour grunt work, I build AI agents that take it off their plate."*

### Formula 2 — Outcome-first
*"I help [ICP] [achieve specific outcome] by [unique mechanism]."*
Example: *"I help service-business founders cut 20 hours of admin a week using custom Claude agents."*

### Formula 3 — Provocation
*"Most [ICP] [common assumption]. I [contrarian approach]."*
Example: *"Most agencies build dashboards. I build the agents that actually run the agency."*

## Pressure-test each option

For each logo candidate, run it through:

1. **Stranger test** — could a person who heard it once repeat it back to a friend?
2. **Search test** — does it use language the ICP types into Google or LinkedIn?
3. **Curiosity test** — does it make them ask a follow-up question?
4. **Voice test** — does it sound like the founder, or like LinkedIn jargon?

A logo that fails any of these gets cut.

## Expanded versions

Once you have a winning 7-second logo, derive:

- **30-second version** — adds the credibility line + soft CTA
- **60-second version** — adds the proof story + hook to next conversation
- **DM opener** — the same idea in a 2-line LinkedIn DM

## Boundaries

- Never longer than 7 seconds. If it doesn't fit, the offer or the ICP is too vague — push back upstream.
- Never use "I help" without saying *who* and *to what specific outcome*.
- Always offer multiple alternatives — the founder must hear it in their own voice.
